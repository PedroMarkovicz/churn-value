## Model details

- **Deployed model:** `lightgbm_seasonal` — LightGBM, tuned with Optuna under rolling-origin cross-validation (mean validation log loss 0.6741), then Platt calibration on the 2011-06-10 cutoff.
- **Inputs:** 19 features computed at the cutoff: base customer history, arithmetic derived features and the cutoff month (context).
- **Output:** calibrated probability that the customer makes no purchase in the next 90 days, used to rank customers by expected retention profit.
- **Provenance:** code `92253155cd30811829a063a6dfb12b607bf7b553`, data SHA-256 `572e36277c23…`, config SHA-256 `b94e3e86505d…`, contract 1.0.0, created 2026-09-27.

## Intended use

- Monthly batch scoring of an online wholesaler's active customers to decide whom to offer a retention incentive, under explicit economic assumptions.
- Not intended for decisions about individuals beyond marketing contact, for other businesses without retraining, or as a causal estimate of what an incentive achieves.

## Data

- UCI Online Retail II (Chen, 2012), CC BY 4.0: a UK online wholesaler, Dec 2009 – Dec 2011.
- Training cutoffs: 2010-06-10 to 2011-03-10 (10 monthly snapshots); calibration 2011-06-10; test 2011-09-10 (1,920 customers, churn rate 30.8%).

## Limitations and risks

- **Seasonality:** the churn rate moves with the calendar and the holdout's label window covers the Q4 peak. Calibration is checked on every later cutoff above.
- **Assumed economics:** the acceptance rate γ and the incentive and acquisition multipliers are assumptions, not measurements; the web app lets users change them.
- **No treatment data:** profit assumes an accepted incentive retains the customer; the model predicts churn, not the effect of contacting.
- **Small population:** a few thousand eligible customers per cutoff; CIs are wide.
- **History-length features drift by construction** (tenure, totals grow as the data ages); their PSI against the training cutoffs is reported, not hidden.

## Ethical considerations

- The data identify customers only by a numeric ID; no personal attributes are used.
- Country is used only as `is_uk`; decisions are commercial offers, not access to services.
